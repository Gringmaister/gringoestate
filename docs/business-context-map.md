# Business Context Map for Panel Integration

## Ambbi
Potential future data blocks:
- buildings
- units
- incidents
- maintenance queue
- cleaning/check-in pipeline
- staff ownership by task

## Gringo Estate
Potential future data blocks:
- properties
- leads
- lead status
- next follow-up
- publication/copy queue
- broker tasks

## Personal / CEO
Potential future data blocks:
- office 10:00 brief
- top 3 priorities
- blockers waiting on third parties
- reminders/follow-ups

## Integration principle
Do not connect generic data just to fill space. Every data source added to the panel should end in either:
- a metric
- a queue
- a next action
